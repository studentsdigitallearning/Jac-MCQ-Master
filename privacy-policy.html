This page applies to the JAC MCQ Master mobile application. It is separate from policies for any other website or service.

1. About JAC MCQ Master

JAC MCQ Master is an educational mobile application intended for students in Classes 8 to 12. It provides multiple-choice question practice, tests, learning progress features and, where available, leaderboard features.

App name: JAC MCQ Master
Package name: com.jacboard.mcqmaster

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features you use and the app version, information may be processed to provide app functionality. This may include class or course selection; test participation details; a display name or temporary roll number where requested; test answers, scores, accuracy, completion time and submission information; and technical information needed to operate, maintain and protect the application. The actual data handling depends on the features enabled in the installed version. Do not enter sensitive personal information into fields that do not request it.

3. Daily Tests and Leaderboard

Where daily test and leaderboard features are enabled, the app may process a student's selected class, temporary participation details and test performance to validate submissions and calculate class-wise rankings. Daily test records and leaderboard information may be retained, reset or deleted according to the app's configured data-management rules. Leaderboard information may be visible to other app users. Avoid using a display name that reveals sensitive personal information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide tests, calculate scores, display progress, operate class-wise rankings, prevent duplicate or invalid submissions, troubleshoot technical issues and maintain the security and reliability of the application.

5. Database and Third-Party Services

The application uses Supabase as part of its backend/database infrastructure. Information processed through that service may be stored or handled under the service provider's systems and policies. The app may also rely on Android system features, including text-to-speech, to read supported question text aloud. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, meet operational requirements, resolve disputes or comply with applicable law. Some daily test or leaderboard information may be subject to scheduled reset or deletion as described in the app.

7. Students and Children

JAC MCQ Master is designed for school students, including users who may be under 18. If you are a parent or legal guardian and have a concern about information associated with a child using the app, please contact us using the email below.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information to enter into optional fields and can stop using the application at any time. For questions or requests about information handled by the app, contact the support email below. We may need sufficient details to identify the relevant record before responding.

9. Permissions

The application may request permissions needed for its features, such as internet access to communicate with online services. Permission availability and use can vary by app version and Android device. You can review app permissions in your device settings.

10. Links to Other Services

The app or this policy page may refer to third-party websites or services. We do not control the content or privacy practices of those external services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available on this page. Please review it periodically.
